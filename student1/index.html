About Taiwan

Taiwan, officially known as the Republic of China (ROC), is an island nation located in East Asia. It is known for its vibrant culture, stunning landscapes, and delicious cuisine.

Top Attractions

  • Taipei 101 - One of the tallest skyscrapers in the world.
  • Taroko Gorge - A breathtaking natural wonder.
  • Sun Moon Lake - A serene and picturesque destination.

Delicious Cuisine

Taiwan is famous for its street food and night markets. Don't miss trying bubble tea, beef noodle soup, and stinky tofu!
